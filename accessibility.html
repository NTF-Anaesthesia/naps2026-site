Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What you can do on this site

  • Use a keyboard to reach every link and button. The item you are on has a clear outline.
  • Zoom the page up to 200% without losing content.
  • Use a screen reader to find and download every model.

Known problems

  • The 3D viewer needs a mouse or touch screen to turn the model. If you cannot use it, you can still download every model from the list.
  • The 3D viewer does not describe the shape of each model in words.

Report a problem

If something on this site does not work for you, email us.
