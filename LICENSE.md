# NAPS 2026 Airway Trainer

## Author
* **Author:** Dr. Koh Wenjun

## Attribution & Source Materials
This trainer incorporates and modifies elements from the following open-source designs:
1. **Overall Design & Inspiration:** Inspired by the *Cricothyroidotomy Trainer* by Kardioversion, available at: https://makerworld.com/en/models/1979684-cricothyrotomy-trainer
2. **Larynx** Modified from the *Upper Airway Model* by flanker1743, available at: https://makerworld.com/en/models/591403-upper-airway-model
3. **Bronchial Tree** Modified from *BodyParts3D* by The Database Center for Life Science, available at: https://lifesciencedb.jp
